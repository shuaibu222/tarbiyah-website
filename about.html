About Our Institution

Discover our rich history, values, and commitment to educational excellence

Our Story

From humble beginnings to becoming a leader in education

Over 35 Years of Excellence

Founded in 1987 by Dr. Habibu Muhammad Gwarzo

Started at his residence for his family and neighbors

Grew into a full community institution: Islamiyya, Adult Education, Islamic Nursery & Primary, Secondary School, Qur’anic Boarding School

Continuous expansion into various educational fields

Our Mission & Values

The foundation of everything we do

Mission

To nurture students who excel academically and morally across Qur’anic, Arabic, English, Sciences, and Arts disciplines.

Vision

To inspire learners with the fear of Allah and the teachings of the Prophet (S.A.W), building upright, disciplined individuals.

Core Values

Excellence, Integrity, Respect, Responsibility, and Compassion guide our daily interactions and decisions.

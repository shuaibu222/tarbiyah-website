Admissions FAQ

Common questions from parents

What is the admission age for Nursery?

Children must be 2 years old by September 1st of the admission year to join our Nursery program. We have three Nursery classes: Playgroup (2-3 years), Nursery 1 (3-4 years), and Nursery 2 (4-5 years).

Are scholarships available?

Yes, we offer merit-based scholarships for exceptional students entering JSS1 and SS1. Scholarship exams are held annually in March. Need-based financial aid may also be available upon request and review.

When does the academic year begin?

Our academic year runs from September to July, divided into three terms: First Term (September-December), Second Term (January-March), and Third Term (April-July).

What are the school hours?

Nursery: 8:00am - 2:30pm
Primary: 7:30am - 2:30pm
Secondary: 7:30am - 3:00pm
Early drop-off from 7:00am is available for all levels.
